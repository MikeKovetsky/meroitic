# Source: F. Ll. Griffith, "Meroitic Studies IV: The Great Stela of Prince Akinizaz", Journal of Egyptian
# Archaeology 4 (1917), pp. 159-173, sections 1 to 21 on pp. 164 and 166-172. Public domain (published 1917;

# article's text layer was not used for the letters.

# One line per section, in the order of the stela; Griffith's sections run on without a gap from l. 1 to l. 42.
# Where he prints two or more sections side by side (§§ 3, 4, 6, 8, 10 on p. 166; §§ 15 and 19 on p. 170;
# §§ 17 and 21 on p. 171), each section is typed whole on its own line. As printed: "(l. N)" is his mark for the
# start of line N of the stela; ":" the word divider; a space without a divider stands as he printed it; [ ] his
# restorations; "." a lost sign; "(?)" his doubt about the word before it. Typed for marks above or below a
# letter: a "?" right after a letter is his question mark printed above that letter; ḫ is his h with a breve
# below (sign 14, today ḫ), ẖ his h with a line below (sign 15, today ẖ), h a plain h. Footnote numbers, his
# italics (they mark where parallel passages differ) and his footnote marks are left out. The comments that
# follow "#" at the end of a line are ours.
