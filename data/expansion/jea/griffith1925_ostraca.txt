# Griffith's ostraca from Faras, Buhen and Dakkah in his transliteration of 1925
#
# Source: F. Ll. Griffith, "Meroitic Studies V", Journal of Egyptian Archaeology 11 (1925), pp. 218-224, plates
# XXVI to XXX. Public domain (published 1925; the author died in 1934). The copy used is archive.org
# in.ernet.dli.2015.70984 (Digital Library of India), which lacks printed pp. 218-219: the article's opening, before
# F.O. 1 (p. 220). Typed by hand from page images of pp. 220-224 rendered at high resolution, sign by sign, on
# 2026-11-17; the article's text layer was not used for the letters. Every h, every s, every letter with a dot below
# and every accent was looked at again on a crop of the line enlarged six to sixteen times.
#
# One line per text: its number, the page, the plates, a tab, then the text as printed. Griffith's marks:
#   (N)      the start of line N of the ostracon (his numbers); in B.O. 1 the two printed lines carry no numbers and
#            (1) (2) are ours
#   :        his word divider; a divider printed with dots one above the other is typed ":" too
#   [ ]      his restorations; ( ) a letter he supplies
#   .        a lost sign; a run of dots a gap
#   -        his hyphen (he joins with it)
#   *        what follows is in the thin second hand (F.O. 12, 13 and others); *ap* is two-sided as printed
#   //       a hatched stretch (damaged)
#   ⍋        the "rectilinear symbol" (clearly not a numeral, his note on F.O. 10); ⍦ the sign "like a branching
#            tree or ear of corn"
#   (?)      his doubt about the numeral or word before it; (sic) his
#   numerals as printed: 6·3, ·4½, 12·1 (his point and his fractions)
# Letters: ê (today o), š (the sign s), plain s (the sign se), ñ (the sign ne), z (today d), ḫ (h with a breve
# below, today ḫ), ẖ (h with a line below, today ẖ), tê (the sign to). A dot below a letter (the combining dot
# below, U+0323) is his mark of a doubtful sign. Footnotes, his italics and his notes are not typed here; what his
# notes doubt or read otherwise is in src/ostraca.py (NOTES), with the places we are unsure of.
# F.O. 38 prints no letters (it refers to Liverpool Annals XI, pl. LXXII, 16) and is not typed.
