# Lead 1: Philae and the Dodecaschoenus

**Answer first.** This is the strongest lead. Eight people of the 3rd century AD are named both in
Meroitic texts and in Demotic and/or Greek texts (a ninth, Tami, in Demotic and Greek), and their titles
line up word for word. The pairings give 24 Meroitic words, titles and names with a known Egyptian or Greek
equivalent, including a three-word phrase: Meroitic `apote-lh Arome-li-s` = Demotic `pꜣ wpte ꜥꜣ n Hrme`, "the great envoy to Rome".
Almost everything needed is online in one open-access book, FHN III (Eide, Hägg, Pierce and Török 1998),
which prints the Meroitic transliterations (from REM) next to new translations of the Demotic and Greek
texts. What is missing is a published translation of any Meroitic text here: the Meroitic sides are
labels, titularies and funerary formulae, not translations of the Demotic.

All transliterations below were copied from the page images of the PDFs, not from OCR.

### REM 0544: the list of viceroys, Faras (FHN III no. 271, p. 1038)

The stela of Makheye lists ten *peseto*s, each followed by the numeral 1. FHN III reads them as Karinakaror,
Netewitar (REM 0278), (Abrato)ye, Khawitaror (REM 0247), Maloton (REM 0277), (...)itanide, (A)bratoye,
Makhe(...), Amanibelile, Tewineye. Abratoye's two entries match his attestations of AD 253 (Ph. 416) and AD
260 (I. Philae II 180). In the local corpus this text is the line containing `pestE krinkrEr l pestE NtewitrEr`.

### REM 0129: "great envoy to Rome", Faras

FHN III p. 1008 cites REM 0129 and 0312 for `apote-lh Arome-li-s`. REM 0129 is Griffith's Inscr. 129 from
Faras (Ashby p. 80 n. 106: "MI 129/6 funerary stela (ššimete mleyotwi)"). Griffith 1911 p. 10 equates the
Meroitic title with Demotic `wpte-ꜥꜣ-n-Hrme`, "great envoy of Rome", citing Inscr. 139/4 and Kar. 112; in
his commentary on Kar. 112 (a damaged Karanog stela, "[envoy] of Rome") he cites Inscr. 129/4. FHN III does
not print REM 0129 or 0312; the text is in the local corpus (see below).

## The Demotic and Greek sides

These are FHN III's transliterations; the English is summarised, because the translation is copyrighted.

1. Ph. 416 (FHN III no. 260, pp. 1000-1010), AD 253, Hadrian's Gate. Line 1 reads `tꜣ wštet n ⸢Pa⸣-sꜥn sꜣ
   Pa-ꞽst ... pꜣ ḳrnjꜣ ꜣkrre n Pr-ꜥꜣ pꜣ wpte ꜥꜣ n Hrme`: "the obeisance of Pasan (Pope: Sasan) son of Paese
   ..., the qêreñ akrere of the King, the great envoy to Rome". The report names King `Tkrrmn`, the general
   of the water `Wyngyꜣ`, the king's son `ꜣbrṭy`, Meroe (`Mrwe`) and Caesar (`Gsrs`). It is dated by
   Trebonianus Gallus (year 3) and by Teqorideamani (years 2 and 3), which fixes this king's reign.
2. Ph. 417 (FHN III no. 261, pp. 1010-1016), about AD 253: Tami, `tꜣ ꜣrbtgyꜣ n ꞽst`, reports ten years as
   measurer; in year 10 `Bk-mty pꜣ ꜣkrre pꜣ šre Ḳrnyꜣ pꜣ mr-mšꜥ n pꜣ mw` sent him north to the Roman *dux*.
3. Ph. 410 (FHN III no. 249, pp. 978-981): Manitawawi (`Mntwe`) and Hornakhtyotef II, "prophets of Isis, the
   qêreñs, the agents of Isis, the agents of the King of the Land of Nubia".
4. Ph. 421 (FHN III no. 245, pp. 968-972), AD 227/8: Wayekiye (A) (`Wygyꜣ`), "chief ritualist of the King of

## In the local corpus (`data/otten/Data/mero-corpus.txt`, `data/texts.jsonl`)

The corpus writes *o* as `E`, *ḫ* as `x`, *ẖ* as `X`, *ñ* as `N`, and often `S` where FHN prints *s*
(`wES` for *Wos*, `pelmES` for *pelmos*); the mapping is not one-to-one, so try several spellings. The
published corpus also swaps personal names, so a name in a corpus line may not be the original. `texts.jsonl` was being rewritten while this was
compiled, so search by string, not by ID.

## What is missing

1. No Meroitic text here is a translation of a Demotic or Greek one. The pairings are of names and titles,
   not of sentences.
2. The Demotic editions (Griffith 1937; Cruz-Uribe 2016) and Pope's revised Ph. 416 are not freely online.
3. REM 0312 and the full REM 0129 were not seen in print; only the corpus lines and FHN III's citation.
4. The Demotic equivalent of *pelmos adblis* "general of the land" was not found.
5. The Meroitic Chamber labels need Griffith 1912 pls XVIII-XXX to check the readings.
