# Readings of MI II nos. 121, 122, 124 (fork b1), from the Table of Seven Graffiti of Philae, p. 50 (leaf n69)

## Table layout (for anyone reading 123 and 125 from the same table)
- Five sections A to E, separated by horizontal rules; each section has one row per graffito, labelled at the right
  edge (A: 95, 96, 121, 122, 123, 124, 125; B: 95, 121, 122, 123, 124, 125, with 95 and 121 EMPTY; C: 95, 121, 122,
  123, 124, 125; D: 122 printed on three lines, then the label 123 on an EMPTY row; E: 122, 123).
- Each text is read A -> B -> C -> D -> E. "end |" at the left of a row = the text stops there.
- Within a row there are often two columns: the right-hand column (aligned on the right, holding the parallel phrase,
  e.g. A: tewiseti, C: wos : pileqeteli) is read first, then the left-hand column.
- Superscript numbers are stone-line numbers placed at the right of the first sign of the new line (RTL), as in the
  rest of the volume. Griffith's "?" are printed above signs; the "sic" seen under row C122 belongs to row C123
  (above its sign); the two "?" seen under row B122 belong to row B123.
- Sign forms on this page: s (closed 3, as in wos, ksbñ) vs m (small hook + long straight descender, as in mey, mdek,
  yetmdelo) vs ẖ (larger angular > top + long descender, as in pẖrse-, Griffith's "Phrs"); k (flat Z top, ksbñ, mdek,
  berkelo) vs p (rounded ε top, pileqe-, perite, penylito).
